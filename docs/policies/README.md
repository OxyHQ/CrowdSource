# Policies

The universal taxonomy, how application policy sets are versioned and published,
and the Oxy conduct policy that decides when a local violation carries a
network-wide consequence.

Nothing is published here yet. Three layers stay separate when it is: what the
material contains (CrowdSource's universal classification), whether that breaks
a given application's rules (the tenant's versioned policy set), and whether the
conduct should affect trust across Oxy (the Oxy conduct policy). Collapsing them
is what makes a moderation service unusable by a second application.
